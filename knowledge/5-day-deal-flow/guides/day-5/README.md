### [company-audit](company-audit.md)
The 15-minute self-diagnostic and the day's spine. 4 steps: track expenses → score 20 questions → review dashboard → read analysis. **4 pillars, equally weighted — 5 questions each, 1–5 scale, 25 points per pillar, 100 total. There are no differential pillar weights**; the sequencing rule is "lowest pillar first, one at a time," with Marketing overriding as the pillar to fix if it ties for lowest ("Sales management does not matter if there are no leads to manage"). Score legend, per pillar / overall: UGLY 5–10 / 20–40, BAD 11–15 / 41–60, GOOD 16–20 / 61–80, NINJA 21–25 / 81–100; "All Pillars Strong" = every pillar 21+. Pillars: Marketing, Sales (Sales Management), Operations, KPIs — Operations Q1 is the only self-scoring question with a printed rubric (net margin: 1 = 0%, 2 = 1–20%, 3 = 21–40%, 4 = 41–50%, 5 = above 50%). Expense tracker has three buckets — Tools & Marketing (Prospectors, Skip Tracing, DataSift, Direct Mail, Bulk Dialer, Bulk SMS, Click-to-Call), Other Expenses (Rent, Office, Utilities, Insurance, Software, Phone/Internet), Team (Acquisitions, Lead Management, Leadership, Virtual Assistants) — with margin markers at 0%/20%/50%/100%; **Prospectors sit under Tools & Marketing on purpose** so tools + marketing + prospecting ÷ deals locked = fully loaded cost per deal. Cash rules: re-audit every 90 days, verify 6 months of runway, expect **90+ days from marketing start to first closed revenue**. Business-type benchmarks: Lifestyle $200K–$500K/yr, 30–50%+ net margin, team 1–2; Growth $500K–$5M+/yr, 20% minimum margin, team 5+. "Realistic ceiling from mastering the system is about 85%. Sam O'Neil runs about 92%: solo, $800K a year on roughly $1,500 a month of marketing spend." The 30/60/90 plan auto-generates **73 tasks** across Days 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 from your scores, phase, and profile. Quarterly Growth Engine (Audit → Plan → Execute → Review → Re-Audit): **3 key OKRs per quarter per division, 5 max**, worked example 25 contracts/year → 2 deals/month minimum; 9 AM–5 PM is income-producing, after 5 PM is infrastructure; the 90+ day cash conversion cycle is why the quarterly clock works. Restates Day 1/Day 2 material: doors-per-deal bands Elite <10 / Strong 10–35 / Volume >35; Knox blind SFR pull 206, AI 90+ 25.3, Absentee + Low Income 34.9, best ZIP 37914 104.9, Absentee alone 105.9, Low Income alone 62.3, distress AND-stacked on the AI score 48.4 vs 30.6 for the score alone; funnel-stage table — First to Market (obituary/pre-probate/probate) ~66 doors per deal, 4–7 month cycle, near-zero saturation; Niche Volume (senior/vacant/absentee stacks) low saturation, hard to reach; Deadline Distress (foreclosure/NOD) fastest and maxed. Two-channel system: a 3-attempt call blitz over 72 hours and the **Mail 01 handwritten letter ($1.75)** start together on Day 1, folders 01–08 run niche CALL/MAIL pairs, folders 09–10 run bulk (6 attempts across Priority 1, Priority 2, Tier 2), FTM never enters bulk. Reactivation timers: **15 days** foreclosure-to-auction, **45 days** probate ("60 loses deals, 30 irritates people"), **90 days** all other FTM, **45 days** Tier 2 not-interested — 20–30% of platform deals come from these.
Worth opening for: all 20 questions verbatim, the expense-tracker line items, the do/avoid list, the 5-stage quarterly engine. Links out to Doors Per Deal (Day 1), `downloads/deal-flow-tech-stack-sop.xlsx`, `downloads/5day-resource-hub-83-resources.xlsx` (83 tools), and the case-study page ("46 real-world examples").

### [hiring-guide](hiring-guide.md)
~25-min read and the densest numbers page of the day. Opening math: 8 working hours, 3 on data + 2 on admin leaves 3 for revenue — **62% of your day producing zero income**. Three hire triggers (Time Ceiling, Data Volume Overflow, Deal Flow Plateau) and one gate: **budget 6 months of the hire's salary without counting on revenue**. The Replacement Ladder, six roles with pay and sourcing region: 1 Data Manager **$500–$700/mo** (Philippines), 2 Prospector/Caller **$750–$1,000/mo** (Latin America, Egypt), 3 Lead Manager **$1,500–$3,000/mo** (Latin America), 4 Closer **$2,000 base + 3–10%** (US), 5 Sales Manager **$5,000–$6,000 + 2–5% override** (US), 6 Operations Manager **$5,000–$7,000 + bonus** (US). Per-role KPIs and hire triggers: Data Manager 200–500 records/day, <5% duplicate rate, daily uploads for FTM / weekly for niche — hire at 2+ hrs/day on data or lists sitting 48+ hours; Caller 150+ dials/day, **1 correct number per 32 dials**, 8–15 meaningful conversations/day, scale trigger 2 Callers per 1 Lead Manager; Lead Manager 50+ follow-ups/day, 3–5 qualified appointments per week, hot every 1–2 days / warm every 15 / cold every 45, hire when lead response time exceeds 1 hour (400% conversion drop after 1 minute); Closer 5–10 offers/day, hire when 2 Lead Managers overflow; Sales Manager — each rep should cover **3–5x their total cost**, attrition under 15% quarterly, hire past 4 sales seats; Ops Manager — **95%+ SOP compliance**, hire at 8+ people. Phase budgets: Phase 1 none, Phase 2 **$500–$1,700/mo**, Phase 3 **$2,500–$5,700/mo**, Phase 4 **$3,500–$8,000+/mo**. Three sourcing channels: **Facebook groups free** (10 named groups incl. VA Workers PH, Egypt Cold Callers — expect **100–200+ applicants within 48 hours**); **Indeed ~$150/hire** ($8–10/day for about 5 days, shortlist 15–20, interview top 5) with a hard rule to post on the country domain, and 7 posting URLs given (ph/mx/eg/co/ar/in/za .indeed.com/hire); **RemoteLatinos** — Marketplace **$97/mo** self-serve or Done-for-You Talent Hunt **$3,000/hire**. SparkHire is named for screening 100+ applicants (no price given). 6-step funnel — "100 candidates pour in at the top… one or two drop out the bottom": post → screen (shortlist 15–20) → **60-second Loom qualifier** (the single best filter; "if someone cannot record a Loom, they cannot do the job") → live interview of the top 5 recorded with **Fireflies.ai** → **1-week paid trial for the top 1–2** at day-one KPI targets (callers 150 dials/day, data managers 200 records) → onboard on SOPs with daily check-ins for the first 2 weeks. 15 qualifying questions (5 per role) with what each tests, incl. "Hot = 2+ pillars, warm = 1 pillar, cold = 0" and the good/bad answer on LM capacity (**50–80 active leads with a system** vs "as many as you need"). Regional rate table (the ladder's US-centric numbers restated by geography): Data Manager PH $500–$700 / LatAm $1,000–$1,300 / Egypt $500–$700; Caller PH & Egypt $750–$1,000, LatAm $1,100–$1,400 base ($1,500–$1,800 with commission); Lead Manager LatAm $1,200–$1,500 base ($1,700–$2,000 with commission), US $3,000–$5,000; Closer LatAm $1,200–$1,500 base + 5–7%, US $2,000 + 3–10%; Sales Manager US $5–6K + 2–5%. Payment stack: **Wise ~$40/mo**, **DocuSign ~$30/mo**. Activity benchmark table: caller dials/day 150+ via **Tally Counter Chrome extension**, correct numbers/day ~5 (at 32:1), LM follow-ups/day 50+, Data Manager records/day 200–500, **not-interested to deal ratio 25–100 contacts = 1 deal**, closer offers/day 5–10. "An underperforming team member costs you 2–3 deals per month in opportunity cost" — coach or replace within 30 days. Lifestyle vs Growth restated with different numbers than company-audit: Lifestyle $200K–$500K/yr, **1–3 people**, **50%+ net margin**; Growth $500K–$5M+/yr, **5–15+ people**, 20% minimum.
Worth opening for: the 10-item onboarding checklist, all 15 qualifying questions with their tests, the 10 Facebook group links, the full regional rate table. Assets: `downloads/jd-data-manager.docx`, `downloads/jd-cold-caller.docx`, `downloads/jd-lead-manager.docx`, `downloads/candidate-intake.skill`; the applicant example videos (5 each for Cold Caller / Setter-LM / Closer) are view-only in Drive. ### [sop-creation](sop-creation.md)
~12-min read built around one claim: **a 40-minute training call, 6,200 words, zero structure → an 11-page SOP**. The Playbook Creator skill extracted, from that one Knox County foreclosure-pull transcript: a **4-phase process map** (Research → Access → Extract → Upload, each phase 1–2 steps with decision gates), **6 numbered steps**, **2 decision trees** ("Is This a Real Foreclosure?" and Property Type Routing — Residential → standard comp workflow, Commercial → skip as out of scope, Land → different valuation), **3 tools identified**, **7 pro tips**, **1 worked example** (All Tassel Pike, Knox County), screenshot placeholders at every critical UI interaction, and a **6-item quality checklist** (data formatting, all fields populated, duplicates removed, property status verified, upload confirmed, screenshot of final list saved) plus a Common Problems table and a printable Quick Reference card. Output constraints named: **Mermaid process maps max 7 nodes**, **5th-grade reading level**. 5-step pipeline with per-step time budgets: 1 Learn the skill (varies, **1–4 hours** for a first attempt; do the task twice before documenting it), 2 Record it in Loom or Fireflies (**20–60 min**; narrate thinking, not clicks), 3 Get the transcript (**2 min**; never edit it first), 4 Feed to Playbook Creator (**3–5 min**), 5 Polish and deploy (**15–30 min**; **screenshots cut training questions by half**). Three capture tools: **Loom** (free tier, primary, auto-transcribes), **Fireflies.ai** (meetings/training calls), and live training + the Teach Claude Chrome extension. **Claude Pro at $20+/mo — no Max plan required.** Library mechanics: naming convention `[ROLE] - [PROCESS] - [VERSION]` (example "Data Manager - Knox County Foreclosure Pull - v2.1"), a top-level Google Drive "SOPs" folder with one subfolder per role and an Archive subfolder, and an **8-step SOP Verification Ladder** ending in a scheduled quarterly SOP review. SOPs by role — 4 roles, 5 SOPs each: Data Manager (priority #1 — county pull per county, skip tracing, list upload/tagging, cleaning/dedup, CRM field mapping), Cold Caller (opening script by list type, objection flowchart for price/timeline/third party/trust, dispositions, voicemail framework, cadence by temperature), Lead Manager (STABM daily routine, appointment scripts, hot/warm/cold cadence, task workflow, status change procedures), KPI Tracker (daily sheet entry, weekly review prep, caller scoring, expense categorization, monthly trend reporting) — each with named cross-references to First-to-Market Data Sourcing, Deep Prospecting, Niche Sequential Marketing, Lead Management & CRM, CRM Tasks, KPI Tracking, and Company Audit. Phase priorities restate pay: Phase 1 Data Manager $500–$700/mo, Phase 2 Lead Manager **$2,000–$3,000/mo**, Phase 3 every role SOP before every job posting, Phase 4 genealogy/heir-identification SOPs as the thing you clone into the next market. Recording quality rules: a **20-minute thorough recording beats a 40-minute unfocused one**; feed three recordings of the same process for a richer SOP; narrate edge cases so they become decision-tree nodes.
Worth opening for: the six-tab anatomy of the generated SOP, the 8-step verification ladder, the four do/not-this recording pairs. Assets: `downloads/playbook-creator.skill` (byte-identical to the installed `.claude/skills/playbook-creator/`), `downloads/example-sop-knox-foreclosures.docx` (the real 11-page output).

### [kpi-tracking](kpi-tracking.md)
20-min read; explicitly "picks up where the audit leaves off." Framing numbers: **80% of REI businesses that stall had enough leads — a tracking problem, not a lead problem**; visibility alone lifts a caller's dial count **15–20%** before you say a word; track leading indicators (dials, conversations, appointments, offers), not only lagging ones. Revenue Reverse-Engineering Calculator: **3 inputs** (monthly revenue goal, average deal profit, close rate) → **6 outputs** (deals, offers, appointments, conversations, dials per month, contracts signed, each broken to per-day/per-week). Sizing rule: one caller does **150–200 dials/day with a power dialer** — divide the monthly dial target by 150 to get headcount. **The funnel ratios, the number-answering core of the page:** **32:1** dials per correct number; **conversation rate 15–20% of dials** (below 15% = a list or number problem, not effort); **5:1** conversations per appointment; **1.5:1** appointments per offer (an offer on roughly 2 of every 3 appointments); **4:1** offers per contract. The linked phone-scoring guide claims Trestle scoring cuts **32:1 down to 8–10:1**. Per-seat scorecards — Cold Caller: 150–200 dials/day, 5–8 conversations/day, 3–5 appointments/day, **5,500+ dials weekly minimum**, 4–6 hours on the dialer/day, cost per lead tracked weekly; the one metric is appointments set per dial hour (200 dials with 1 appointment is worth less than 120 dials with 4). Lead Manager: 150 dials/day floor, 25 conversations/day, 15–20% conversation rate, **2–5 Sent to Acquisitions/day (the money metric)**, 100% same-day contact on new leads, 0 leads missing tasks, speed to first contact **under 1 minute** (400% higher conversion). Closer: 5–10 offers/day, hot leads contacted within 1 hour, **offer-to-contract 25%+ target — below 20% means your comps are off**. The Google Sheet ("DataSift REI KPI Sheet") is walked tab by tab — **10 tabs**: Start Here, Goals & Setup, Expenses, Monthly Data, Dashboard, Review Dashboard, Caller Tracker, Lead Manager, Transactions, Direct Mail (direct mail costed at **$0.50–$2.00 per piece**). Caller Tracker anti-gaming split: **blue columns (Dials, Leads, DNC) are admin-only from the dialer; yellow columns (Time Spent, Campaign) are caller self-reported**. The page also announces the replacement canonical template — 9 tabs (Leads Summary, First to Market, Lead Management, Acquisition, SmrtDialer, Exhausted, Offers Summary, Costs, Expenses) rolled up by an **8-stage Executive Summary funnel** (Leads Generated, Qualified, Appointments, Offers, Accepted, Contracts, Under Contract, Profit), shipping with a **daily KPI Slack bot that posts at 5 PM on weekdays** inside the DataSift Lead Management plugin (the Day 4 asset); the tab walkthrough still shows the older layout. Tally Counter method: free Chrome extension, **4 counters** (Dials, Correct Numbers, Conversations, Appointments) per caller, transfer to the sheet **daily, never weekly** — and sanity-check against DataSift, because conversations can never exceed dials and **when tally and platform disagree, the platform wins**. Review cadence checklist, 13 items: **Daily 30 minutes** (6 items), **Weekly 1 hour** (4), **Monthly 2 hours** (3). Teams of 3+ get automated Basecamp/Slack check-ins; **DataBox $50+/mo** is the optional real-time visualization that refreshes every 5 minutes. A full copy-paste Claude prompt builds a live 9-panel dashboard (goal gauge, north-star cards, path to goal, the 8-stage funnel, unit economics, monthly trend with a 3-month moving average, marketing efficiency/ROAS, efficiency ratios, full monthly table) from the sheet via the Google Drive connector — Chart.js, single self-contained HTML, ~2-minute setup. KPI priority by phase: P1 **cost per conversation**, P2 **speed to lead**, P3 **cost per contract**, P4 **profit margin trend month over month with a 20%+ net margin goal** — and at P4, "one AI-powered virtual system (Claude + Zapier + DataSift) can replace separate Data Manager and Marketing Manager hires."
Worth opening for: the full dashboard prompt verbatim, the tab-by-tab sheet walkthrough, the manual-sheet vs platform-dashboard comparison ("an auto-populated number gets a glance; a number you type by hand, you feel"). Assets: `downloads/kpi-tracking-template.xlsx`, `downloads/kpi-engine.skill` (byte-identical to the installed `.claude/skills/kpi-engine/`), Tally Counter at tallycount.app.

## How the set fits together + consistency notes

Day 5 is the operating system for everything Days 1–4 produced: company-audit scores the business and hands you one bottleneck, hiring-guide fills the seat that bottleneck implies, sop-creation documents the job before the job posting goes up (the SOP-First Rule — "never hire before your processes are documented"; hiring-guide defers to it explicitly), and kpi-tracking is the instrument panel that tells you whether the seat is working. Each page points at the others by name: kpi-tracking opens by sending you to the audit first and hands its expense analysis back to the audit's tracker; sop-creation cross-references KPI Tracking and Company Audit per role; hiring-guide's resource list ends on the audit and the Replacement Ladder. Constants held across the day: 150 dials/day floor and 32:1 dials per correct number, 15–20% conversation rate, 2–5 Sent to Acquisitions/day as the money metric, 5–10 closer offers/day, 400% conversion within 1 minute, 20–30% of deals from not-interested follow-up, 6 months of runway before any hire, 90+ days to first revenue, re-audit every 90 days, 2 Callers per 1 Lead Manager, Data Manager as first hire at $500–$700/mo, and Claude Pro at $20+/mo as the only AI spend named all day.

Internal wobbles worth knowing:
- **Weekly dial target vs daily.** kpi-tracking sets the Cold Caller at 150–200 dials/day AND a "weekly dial minimum 5,500+". 5,500 ÷ 5 days = 1,100/day. The two cannot both be right; treat 5,500 as an aspirational/multi-caller figure until the sheet is opened.
- **Caller conversations/day.** hiring-guide says 8–15 meaningful conversations/day; kpi-tracking's Cold Caller scorecard says 5–8. Neither reconciles with the page's own "conversation rate 15–20% of dials" (which at 150 dials implies 22–30) — the 15–20% rule is stated for the Lead Manager, whose 25 conversations on 150 dials does fit at ~17%.
- **Caller appointments.** The Cold Caller scorecard lists 3–5 appointments/day against 5–8 conversations/day, but the funnel's own 5:1 conversations-per-appointment ratio would yield 1–1.6. Separately, hiring-guide gives the Lead Manager 3–5 qualified appointments **per week** against the caller's 3–5 **per day**.
- **Lead Manager pay, four different numbers.** hiring-guide's ladder says $1,500–$3,000/mo (LatAm); its own regional table says LatAm $1,200–$1,500 base ($1,700–$2,000 with commission) and US $3,000–$5,000; sop-creation's Phase 2 says $2,000–$3,000/mo; Day 4's lead-management guide says $1,000–$1,500/mo. Day 2's team-structure ($1,500–$3,000) matches only the ladder.
- **Closer pay** splits the same way: ladder and US column $2,000 base + 3–10%, LatAm column $1,200–$1,500 base + 5–7%.
- **Lifestyle business shape.** company-audit: 30–50%+ net margin, team of 1–2. hiring-guide: 50%+ net margin, 1–3 people. Growth team size is 5+ in one and 5–15+ in the other.
- **Direct mail cost.** kpi-tracking prices mail at $0.50–$2.00/piece; company-audit prices the Mail 01 handwritten letter at $1.75; Day 1's pendulum-theory taught $0.50–$0.75. The handwritten piece is the expensive end, not a contradiction, but do not quote a single mail number across days.
- **Case-study count:** 305+ stories (case-studies) vs "46 real-world examples" (company-audit's card for the same link).
- **Agency naming:** `downloads/README.md` lists Ninja Assistants among the tools linked on the Day 5 hub sheet, but **no guide mentions Ninja Assistants** — the only agency taught in the written guides is RemoteLatinos ($97/mo marketplace, $3,000/hire). SparkHire is named twice with no price given.
- **Pillar weighting:** if anyone asks for the audit's "pillar weights," the answer is that there are none — 25 points each, evenly. The weighting is behavioral (fix the lowest first, Marketing wins ties), not arithmetic.
