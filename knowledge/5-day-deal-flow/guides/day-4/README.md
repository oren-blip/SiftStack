### [lead-management](lead-management.md)
The 25-min sales-engine anchor. Speed to lead: calling within 1 minute = 400% higher close rate (HBR); lead-to-deal 1:50 conservative, 1:10 top-tier; lead manager attempt target 50/day. The 4 Pillars (Reason, Timeline, Condition, Price) set a starting temperature — Price: Hot ≤80% of Zestimate, Warm 80–100%, Cold >100%; Timeline: Hot <30 days, Warm 1–3 months, Cold 3–12 months — but temperature IS the follow-up frequency, not a feeling. Cadence table: No Contact daily for 3–5 days → Nurture weekly for 3–6 months (a task stage, not a status); Hot every 2 days, Warm every 15, Cold every 45, Ghosting/Not Interested quarterly. Healthy pipeline ≈ 75% Cold / 15% Warm / 5% Hot. Ghosting drip = 45-day cycle, 4 touches, ~142 days; Not Interested = 14-day buffer then 90-day cycle × 4 (~1 year), each cycle SMS + manual call task; 20–30% of all platform deals come from not-interested leads. Roles: Lead Manager $1K–1.5K/mo (NOTE: Day 2's team-structure guide says $1,500–3,000), 150 dials/day floor, 25 conversations/day, 15–20% conversation rate, 2–5 sent to Acquisitions/day, 100% same-day contact, 0 leads without tasks; Closer $2K base + 3–10% commission, 5–10 offers/day, 2:1 LM-to-closer ratio, hire only when 2 LMs overflow. Default stack: 5 SiftLine boards, 10 active statuses, 26–27 pre-built sequences. Losing cold leads to competitors → tighten 45 → 30 days in 15-day increments.
Worth opening for: the full status-by-status lifecycle with owners, the push-back loop (temperature tag preserved on ghost), the 4 objections (Price/Time/Third Party/Trust), hour-by-hour LM and Closer schedules, the $45K lost-deal story. Assets: `downloads/datasift-lead-management.plugin`, `downloads/lead-manager-coach.skill` (page-only bonus).

### [lead-intake-custom-fields](lead-intake-custom-fields.md)
The intake call as 7 custom-field folders / 75 fields — the fields ARE the offer (a skipped roof + back-taxes field closed a deal $18K below what they should have paid). Folder counts in call order: Qualifying Questions 8, Property Condition 10, CapEx Assessment 17, Debts & Encumbrances 17, Title & Ownership 8, Deal Intelligence 7, Appointment & Next Steps 8. Two folders drive MAO: CapEx (6 systems — roof, HVAC, water heater, plumbing, electrical, junk removal — each with Condition/Age/Notes; every "Unknown" forces a Full-Gut assumption, three Unknowns can push the offer under the deal floor; junk removal options carry estimates: significant $3,000–5,000+, minor $1,500) and Debts (every debt off MAO dollar-for-dollar — worked example: $40K mortgage + $6K back taxes + $2K HOA arrears = $48K straight off). The DataSift Lead Management plugin auto-builds all 7 groups + 75 fields via POST to apiv2.reisift.io in ~60 seconds. API quirk: `text` = multi-line, `text_input` = single-line (backwards from intuition).
Worth opening for: the complete 75-field reference table (field, type, what it tells you), the CapEx cheat sheet with dropdown options per system, the MAO derivation walkthrough. Asset: `downloads/datasift-lead-management.plugin` (also builds the training manual + KPI bot; matches this repo's `build-custom-fields` skill).

### [crm-events](crm-events.md)
Events = the scheduling engine, not a calendar. 4 tabs (All Events, Appointments, Tasks, Completed); 4 appointment types (Walkthrough, Contract Signing, Inspection, Other — completion requires an outcome; Offer Information is updated separately); 3 default task preset groups (Lead Management 6 tasks, Acquisitions 3, Transactions 3), all initially assigned to "Sensei" (account owner) — teams must reassign by User/Role/Round Robin. Recurrence: daily/weekly/bi-weekly/monthly + Skip Weekends. The default account build: 6 SiftLine boards here (Lead Mgmt, Acquisitions, Transactions, Wholesale, Flips, Rentals — the other two CRM guides count 5 with Wholesale/Flips merged), 10 active statuses, 26 pre-built sequences all triggering on "New Lead" status, 5 saved filter presets. Restricted roles (Prospectors, Acquisitions, Dispositions, Researchers) see only assigned records — always pair task assignment with record assignment. Sequence limits: Professional 8, Business+ unlimited; sequences cannot trigger sequences; 4 roles can create them (Sensei, Super Admin, Admin, Marketer). Lifecycle numbers restated: 400% within 1 minute, No Contact daily 3–5 days, Nurture weekly 3–6 months, Hot 2 / Warm 15 / Cold 45 days, Not Interested probate 45 / auction 15 / general 90 days, 20–30% of deals from not-interested. Custom preset guidance: 3–6 tasks per group; worked examples (Wholesale 4 tasks incl. buyer follow-up at 24 hours; Rentals 5 tasks at 0/3/7/14/45 days).
Worth opening for: the 8-item configuration checklist, the role-visibility matrix, the status-by-status event map, the three custom-preset recipes. (The sheet's un-linked "Default Account Setup REISift" doc is summarized by this page.)

### [crm-sequences](crm-sequences.md)
Sequence anatomy: exactly 1 trigger per sequence, optional AND-logic conditions, 1+ immediate actions — 10 trigger types, 13 conditions, 12 actions (Status Change + Tags Added cover 80% of use cases). Hard rules: sequences cannot chain (A's action never fires B's trigger); tags applied during upload do NOT fire "Tags Added"; a task action with "Assign to property assignee" on an unassigned record silently creates nothing. Accounts created after April 16, 2025 ship 26 pre-built sequences in 5 folders, all firing on status → "New Lead" (the outbound/inbound line: before New Lead = marketing, after = lead management). Plan limits: Essentials (legacy) 3, Professional 8, Business ($299/mo) unlimited — the guide names the exact 8 to pick on Professional (New Lead assignment/card, Ghosting Nurture 45-day 4-touch drip, Not Interested 90-day + 14-day buffer × 4 cycles, Hot alert, Offer Sent, Under Contract, Closed archive). SMS actions need smrtPhone/Twilio/Plivo (Kixie, Smarter Contact, Launch Control unsupported); email needs Gmail; all sends hold to the 8 AM–9 PM account-timezone window. Mandatory safeguard: every receiving sequence on re-entry MUST include "Remove from Drip Campaign". Phase 3 notes: Data Manager $500–700/mo first hire, ~2 callers per lead manager.
Worth opening for: the trigger/condition/action "when to use each" catalog, the 7-step creation walkthrough, the 6-failure troubleshooting table (bell-icon silent failures), folder conventions.

### [crm-tasks](crm-tasks.md)
Execution layer (18-min read): 3 task entry points (always create from within the record — Events-page creation costs 10 extra seconds/task), 4 assignment methods (self, Round Robin, self+role-notes, role/custom group). The #1 silent failure restated with a role table: 4 restricted roles see the task but not the record. Deadline pattern library: Hot = every 2 days, first attempt within 1–2 hours, Skip Weekends OFF; Warm = 15 days, Cold = 45 days, both Skip Weekends ON; No Contact = daily 3–5 days at 3 attempts/day (part of the 27-touch grid), then weekly Nurture for 3–6 months. Preset pyramid: Layer 1 defaults (cover 90% of Phase 1 needs), Layer 2 market-customized, Layer 3 custom (3–6 tasks per group). KPIs: 95%+ task completion (Yellow 80–95%), <5% overdue (zero at EOD), Hot worked within 2 hours / Warm 48 hours / Cold 7 days, 40–60 tasks/day per caller. LM daily benchmarks: 150 dials (floor), 25 conversations, 15–20% conversation rate, 2–5 sent to AQS (THE money metric), 100% same-day contact, 0 leads missing tasks. Phase targets: P1 20–30 tasks/day self-assigned; P3 Sales Manager $5,000–6,000/mo audits overdue daily, ~2 callers per LM; P4 10–15 deep-research tasks/day, Data Manager $500–700/mo, 3–4 custom presets max.
Worth opening for: the 5-step 10-minute morning triage (the "T" in STABM), the working-vs-broken record-assignment comparison, the completion-cascade diagram, per-phase task strategies.

### [drip-campaigns-deep-dive](drip-campaigns-deep-dive.md)
Drips vs sequences: sequences fire instantly, drips add configurable delays (minutes 0–59, hours 1–23, days 1–365); 26 pre-built sequences vs ZERO pre-built drips (by design). 80% of motivated sellers need 5+ follow-ups. Three design rules: drips supplement (never replace) manual follow-up; receiving sequences MUST "Remove from Drip Campaign"; no task conflicts. Only exited-pipeline leads get drips — the two canonical campaigns: Ghosting Nurture (45-day cycle, 4 SMS, ~142 days total) and Not Interested Nurture (14-day buffer + 90-day cycle × 4 = ~360 days, each cycle SMS + manual call task); Dead leads get NO drip and no task; Cold leads are NOT on drips. Six copy-paste templates with full SMS copy: 1–2 canonical, 3 Probate 45-day (3 SMS + 1 task), 4 Foreclosure 15-day (3 SMS), 5–6 non-standard (Warm 30-day steps; Speed-to-Lead at 0/+24h/+48h). Cadence guide: foreclosure 15 / probate 45 / general 90 / dead 90 days. Mechanics: SMS ~$0.01/message, keep under 150 characters, minute-chains 2 steps max (3+ messages in an hour reads aggressive), 8 AM–9 PM send window, carrier must be smrtPhone/Twilio/Plivo, A2P 10DLC compliance required, setup under 5 minutes. Phase 1: build Ghosting first, add Not Interested after 50+ not-interested dispositions. 4 monitoring statuses (Active/Completed/Failed/Removed) — review Failed weekly, it's a data-quality signal.
Worth opening for: all six template scripts verbatim, the delay ladder, enrollment paths (manual filter-select-send vs sequence-attached — the Mara Garcia workflow), debugging table. Asset: `downloads/drip-campaign-sop.pdf` ("Drip Campaign V2" on the sheet).

### [comping-workflow](comping-workflow.md)
The Claude comping skill (≈25 iterations, deliberately conservative — ARV is the floor) compresses 45–90 min of comping into one conversation and a 7-tab Excel workbook; needs only Claude Pro $20/mo. Two-Bucket Method: Bucket A unrenovated / Bucket B renovated (sold within 90 days, 6 months max, arm's-length); Market Premium = (PPSF_B − PPSF_A) / PPSF_A — typical 10–30%, re-examine below 5% or above 30% (≤5% = flipping doesn't pay). Disclosure (38 states) confidence ±2–5%; non-disclosure (12 states) triangulation ±5–7%: last-list-price by DOM (<7 DOM = list/101%, 7–30 = 97–100%, >30 = 90–95%), deed-of-trust math (Conventional loan ÷ 0.80, FHA ÷ 0.965, VA ÷ 1.00), tax-ratio sanity check. Worked example 5532 Joyce Ann Dr, Dayton OH (1961 ranch, 3/2, 1,892 sqft): 6 comps in 2 miles/90 days/built 1955–1975/GLA 1,400–2,150; Bucket A median PPSF $125.03 (4 comps), Bucket B $156.35 (2 comps), renovation premium 20.4%; adjustments GLA +$78,000, condition −$3,000, basement +$10,000; ARV math $125.03 → +20.4% → $150.53 → −2% sentiment → $147.52 × 1,892 = $279,108 − $14,000 features = **$265,000 ARV** (±5%, $252–278K); market: median $225,000, PPSF $131, 34 DOM, 98% sale-to-list, 3.5 months inventory; wholesale target $205–215K; GLA discrepancy flag (1,652 vs 1,892 sqft → ARV drops to ~$240K, wholesale ~$195K). Adjustment cheatsheet: bedroom +$10K under $500K / +$25K over (NOTE: the local `real-estate-comping.skill` was corrected to $5K to match `comp_analyzer.py` — the hub guide still teaches $10K); full bath ±$10K, half ±$5K; garage $10–15K ($20–25K hot/cold climates), carport $5–7.5K; backing busy road −$10K, fronting −$20K; pool hot climate +$20–40K, cold +$5–15K; extra 5,000 sqft lot $5–10K / $30–50K; water view +$20–50K / +$50–150K; basement value 10–15% of PPSF unfinished up to ~50% finished; ADU 50% not-deeded / 100% deeded; foundation −$5K to −$40K+. Calibration: feed 3–5 closed deals from the same market.
Worth opening for: all 7 tab walkthroughs with real numbers, the full adjustment tables, SiftMap polygon rules (never cross major roads), calibration do/don't. Assets: `downloads/real-estate-comping.skill`, `downloads/comp-report-5532-joyce-ann.xlsx`.

### [rehab-estimator-workflow](rehab-estimator-workflow.md)
Companion to comping (comp first — the renovation premium picks the finish tier): the Rehab Estimator skill (4 iterations) turns inspection photos + comp report into a 9-tab Excel workbook. Uncalibrated it runs 20–40% above contractor bids; after 3–5 closed deals with real SOWs it lands within 10–15% (the guide later claims within 5%). Four finish tiers, comps pick the tier: 1 Builder $15–25/SF (<$100K ARV), 2 Mid $25–40 ($100–200K), 3 Investor-Flip $35–60 ($200–400K, most common), 4 Retail-Premium $50–80+/SF ($400K+). Same 5532 Joyce Ann property: full rehab **$63,379** ($33.50/SF; subtotal $57,617 + 10% contingency $5,762 — top lines: Windows & Doors $9,775, Bathrooms $8,500, Flooring $8,090, Kitchen $8,008, Paint $7,795), wholetail **$11,358** ($6.00/SF, 5% contingency); the contractor-actual read $55,218 ÷ 1,892 SF = $29.19/SF, low end of Tier 3 because systems were functional (Dayton labor multiplier 0.82x; national range 0.7x rural to 1.5x metro). Deal economics: ARV $265,000, purchase $155,000 → full-rehab profit $46,621 / 21.3% ROI, wholetail $48,642 / 29.2% ROI, 75%-Rule MAO $135,371; profit-per-month test ($25K in 2 months = $12,500/mo beats $45K in 5 = $9,000/mo); wholetail should cost 15–30% of full rehab, $5–15/SF, 2–3-month hold vs 4–6. Methodology rules: room-by-room not category (transitional spaces add $3,000–4,000); paint as one $2.90–3.00/SF rate (splitting it tripled V1 paint $7,763 → $16,878); never forget appliances (V4 missed $2,950); outlets/switches $100/room; 60–65% rule (materials = 60–65% of total; >65% labor underpriced, <50% over-scoped; $25–35/SF flip → materials $15–23/SF); >$40/SF on a cosmetic flip = over-scoping. Adjusters: material availability ±5–15% (50 vs 100+ miles from a distribution hub), permits $500–3,000 (kept as a separate line), Q4/Q1 cold-climate labor +10–15%; HVAC replace if >15 years.
Worth opening for: all four tier spec tables (materials, brands, price points), the 9-tab walkthrough with the real workbook numbers, the 5-week project checklist, calibration format. Assets: `downloads/rehab-estimator.skill`, `downloads/rehab-estimate-5532-joyce-ann.xlsx`, `downloads/investor-bootz-sample-inspection-report.pdf` (Investor Bootz inspections ~$75–100/report).

### [buyer-prospecting](buyer-prospecting.md)
Build the buyers list BEFORE the deal (a list built early turns the 60-day cliff into a 2-day assignment). Four buyer types by hold time: Wholesale <72 hours, Wholetail <90 days (resells 10–25% above purchase), Flip 90 days–18 months (highest price tolerance), Buy-and-Hold 18+ months (5+ purchases, no matching sales, entity names). Two SiftMap presets, both 18-month lookback: Recent Active Investors (3+ purchases; needs SiftMap Pro $297/mo, included in Expert $499+) and Quick Resale/Flippers (<12-month resale; all plans). The 3-Signal Read: Volume, Velocity, Recency (10 properties in 6 months ≠ 10 in 6 years; last buy 3 years ago = probably stopped). Worked example SMP Holdings LLC: $63,000 cash purchase, $442,000 portfolio at 100% equity. The Buyer Prospector skill (Claude Pro $20+/mo): nationwide database of 84,000+ records across 1,471 counties; Knox County TN = 138 active buyers → 112 LLCs, 11 trusts, 9 corporations, 5 individuals; 84% decision-maker identification rate; 3-tab Excel output (Found / Not Found / All Records); strip iBuyers and institutionals — 20 known local buyers beat 200 random cash-buyer names. Then prospect the list with niche sequential (cold call first, text never first) and only verified buyers enter the Phonebook (4 contact statuses: Buyer, Contractor, Lender, Agent). Buyer Finder AI (0–100 score, price recommendations) expected in 3–6 months; today's manual workflow ≈ 2–4 hours/county.
Worth opening for: the SiftMap spot-the-buyer-type patterns, the Phonebook hygiene do/don't, the prospect-then-verify flow. Assets: `downloads/buyer-prospector.skill`, `downloads/buyer-analysis-example.xlsx`.

## How the set fits together + consistency notes

Day 4 is the post-marketing half of the machine: Day 2's outreach produces a raised hand, the status flips to New Lead (the outbound/inbound line), and the CRM Quartet takes over — lead-management sets the temperatures and cadences, lead-intake-custom-fields captures the 75 answers that price the deal, crm-events/crm-tasks run the daily execution, crm-sequences wires the automation, drip-campaigns catches the exits. Comping → rehab → buyer-prospecting is the deal-analysis chain (comp first; the 20.4% renovation premium is the hinge value reused in both analysis guides, on the same 5532 Joyce Ann property end to end). Constants held across all guides: Hot 2 / Warm 15 / Cold 45-day cadences, No Contact daily 3–5 days then weekly Nurture 3–6 months, Ghosting 45-day × 4 (~142 days), Not Interested 14-day buffer + 90-day × 4 (~360 days), niche timers 15/45/90, 20–30% of deals from not-interested, 400% within 1 minute, 150 dials/day floor, 2–5 sent to AQS/day, 8 AM–9 PM send window, Professional = 8 sequences / Business $299 = unlimited, 26 pre-built sequences + 10 statuses.

Internal wobbles worth knowing:
- **Board count:** crm-events says 6 default SiftLine boards (Wholesale and Flips separate); crm-sequences and lead-management say 5 (Wholesale/Flips merged).
- **Lead Manager pay:** lead-management says $1K–1.5K/mo; Day 2's team-structure says $1,500–3,000/mo.
- **Bedroom comp adjustment:** the hub guide teaches +$10K (under $500K); this repo's skill library was deliberately corrected to $5K to match `comp_analyzer.py` (April 2026). The guide is the newer public teaching — flag before "fixing" either.
- **Rehab $/SF:** the skill's estimate is $33.50/SF ($63,379) but the contractor-actual math shown is $29.19/SF ($55,218) — the guide uses both without reconciling.
- **Calibration accuracy:** stated as "within 10–15%" up top and "within 5%" at the bottom of rehab-estimator-workflow.
