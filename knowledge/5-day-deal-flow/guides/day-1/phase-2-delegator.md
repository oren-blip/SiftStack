## Follow-ups first. Fresh records last.

    Run the same order every morning and you never double-dial a record. Closest to a deal goes first. Never-called goes last.

      The magazine feeds from the bottom. Attempt 3 records fire first, fresh records load underneath and wait their turn.

- Follow-ups and rehash. Every scheduled callback, plus the not-interested list. Worked daily, before any cold dial.

- Attempt 3 records. Today is their fourth and final attempt. Convert them or move them to mail.

- Attempt 2 records. Third attempt today. Call every number on the record again.

- Attempt 1 records. Second attempt today. Voicemail and a personalized text again.

- Ready to call. Fresh records enter last, so today's new list never collides with yesterday's.

      One full attempt

      Call every number on the record. Leave a voicemail. Send a personalized text. Then increment the attempt counter yourself, manually, so the count stays honest.

      Four full attempts, then the record moves to the mail list.

## Calls chase the reachable. Mail reaches the rest.

    Mail is the second wave, with one exception: the skip-traced list that returned zero phone numbers gets mail first.

    **Mail the no-number list before anything else.** No investor can call those owners. Mail is the only door in, and you are alone in the mailbox at $0.50-0.75 per piece.

    Everyone else earns mail by not answering. After the fourth full attempt, the record moves to the mail list. Alternate the mailer type on every send so touch five never looks like touch one.

    The text touch stays attached to the phones: every call attempt ends with a personalized text at a penny each.

      Smarter Contact sends the personalized text that ends every call attempt. One cent per message.

## Obituary lists run on one rule set. No exceptions.

    Only about 30% of deceased owners ever file probate. The obituary list reaches the other 70% no probate marketer will ever see.

        Immediately

        Mail the family

        A respectful letter goes out the day you find the obituary. Mail is the only first touch allowed.

        3+ months after filing

        First call

        No phone contact until the probate filing is at least 3 months old. Set the date in the CRM and wait it out.

        Never

        Cold text

        Not on any sensitive list. Not once. One cold text can burn the least-competed list in your county.

    That is why obituary beats waiting for probate. You reach the family respectfully first, and 70% of these estates never appear in probate records at all.

## Three numbers decide when you hand off the phones.

    Track dials, answer rate, correct numbers, and leads every day. When leads outrun the hours you can give them, Phase 3 starts.

      150
Dials per day
The full-time caller benchmark. Pace your own hours against it.

      4
Attempts per record
Every record earns four full attempts before it moves to mail.

      75-100
Not-interesteds per deal
That many correct-number not-interesteds hold about one deal inside 12 months.

    A correct-number not-interested is inventory, not a loss. Log it, drip it, and call it back. The 12-month payoff only shows up if the list gets worked daily.

## Graduate on overflow. Never on ambition.

    The trigger is leads arriving faster than you can hold them. Not a revenue target, not a calendar date, not boredom.

    Watch for two slip signals: follow-ups sliding a day, and new leads waiting for a first call. Both mean you are leaking deals. Bank 3-6 months of the next hire's cost, then move.
