## AI Data Is Not Better Leads. It Is Fewer Leads Worth More.

    Most investors hear "AI scoring" and think: more leads, better conversion, instant deals. That is not how it works.

      The AI/Investor Score is the lead lever of the Data Priority Pyramid. Pull it and be marketing the same day, no build-out. It flags a property before it becomes an obvious, marketed list: the lowest-saturation data you can work.

      On live SiftMap supply, the Investor Score runs 30.6 doors per deal at 80+ and 22.8 at 90+, against 218.1 for baseline unscored single-family. The 90-plus band surfaces roughly $5.7M to $7.2M gross per 1,000 doors worked, at 56 to 58 percent margins.

      Step the score threshold up and the street shortens. Same deal, 218 doors down to 30.

    **The sequencing principle:** lead with the AI-score precision pull, run the native distressor lists in parallel. Never AND-stack Standard Distress on the score (48.4 doors per deal versus 30.6 alone). Add the First-to-Market courthouse layer once precision is producing. See the Data Priority Pyramid guide.

## How AI Scoring Works

    Four stages from raw data to actionable list. Every property in your target area gets a score. You choose the threshold.

    The score rarely drops drastically month-to-month on properties already above 50. Once a property is flagged as high-probability, it tends to stay flagged. So your AI-filtered list remains stable. You are not chasing a moving target.

        One county, one list, sequential marketing live. Same-day FTM is the on-ramp to the AI score.

#### Phase 2: The Delegator Bridge Option

        Data and admin off your plate. Expert plan standard. AI add-ons available.

#### Phase 3: The Manager Primary Audience

        Team scaled on overflow, bulk volume. AI plan is the enterprise tier.

#### Phase 4: The Owner Depth Amplifier

        Deep-prospecting every unreached door. AI add-ons enhance the deep motion.

## How AI Scoring Connects to Everything Else

    AI scoring is not a standalone tool. It plugs into five parts of your deal flow system. Click each connection to see how.

#### Data Priority Pyramid: The Lead Lever

      The AI/Investor Score leads the pyramid: market it the same day, no build-out, with the acute native distressor lists as parallel pulls. First-to-Market courthouse data ($500-$2,000 per contract once built) is the consistency layer you add once precision is producing.

      **Read the full Data Priority Pyramid guide →**

#### SiftMap Filtering: AI Score 50+ Layer

      In SiftMap, the AI score filter is the final layer after base criteria (SFR, equity, ownership) and distressors. A minimum score of 50 cuts your list by roughly half; everything below 50 goes into a secondary hold-and-watch pool.

      **See the complete SiftMap filter setup →**

#### Bulk Sequential Marketing: AI Data as Source

      AI-filtered lists of 5,000-25,000+ records feed directly into your bulk sequential marketing pipeline. The power dialer handles volume. The AI filter handled quality. You are calling half the people and reaching a higher percentage of motivated sellers.

      **See the bulk sequential marketing flow →**

#### Sold Properties: AI Validation Layer

      Sold Properties shows the AI score each property had at time of sale: your validation loop. Filter by score 50-100 for the last 6 months and analyze what sold: price points, distressors, characteristics. The distressors present at sale tell you which lists to pull next.

      **Learn the Sold Properties validation workflow →**

#### Phone Scoring: Trestle on AI-Filtered Lists

      After AI filtering cuts your list from 25,000 to 12,500, run those numbers through Trestle phone scoring ($0.015/number). Score 81-100 goes to your call team first; score 0-20 gets dropped. The best numbers on the best properties.

## AI Scoring in SiftMap

    The AI score filter is one dropdown in SiftMap. Set your threshold, and half your list disappears. The good half stays.

      In SiftMap, navigate to the AI Scores section in the filter panel. Set the minimum to 50 and the maximum to 100 for whichever score type matches your strategy. Investor Off-Market for outbound wholesaling. Realtor for listing prediction. On-Market for MLS offer systems.

      The filter works alongside every other SiftMap criterion. So your full stack looks like: base criteria (SFR, equity, ownership) plus distressors (senior, tax delinquent, vacant) plus AI score 50+. Each layer narrows. AI is the final precision cut.

## AI Readiness Scorecard

    Five things need to be true before AI scoring is a smart investment. Read them as a checklist, not a quiz.

    **Data motion.** You already pull and work fresh lists (SiftMap or same-day First-to-Market) consistently.

    **List size.** Your lists are large enough that AI filtering still leaves workable volume.

    **Marketing budget.** You can sustain $499-$1,250 a month for data on top of existing marketing spend.

    **Team capacity.** You have callers and lead managers ready to work AI-filtered lists.

    **Validation habit.** You regularly check Sold Properties to validate your targeting.

    All five true? Lead with the Investor Score as your primary precision pull and run acute distressor lists in parallel. Not yet? Work fresh same-day First-to-Market pulls as your low-saturation on-ramp, then move onto the score as soon as deal flow allows.
