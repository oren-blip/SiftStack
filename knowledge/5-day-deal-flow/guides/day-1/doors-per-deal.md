## Effort is half the picture. Payoff is the other half.

    Plot effort against median gross and the traps show themselves.

      Every high-confidence Knox list and combo. Left means less effort, higher means more profit. The two hollow points are the traps.

    **The negative-margin trap.** Bad Credit + Out-of-State runs 10.4 doors per deal and loses $5k per deal. Sharp is not the same as profitable. Read both axes.

    **The small-sample mirage.** Notice of Default reads 13.6 doors and $153k gross from 8 deals. Directional, not bankable. Act on high-confidence rows first.

## Three levers move the number.

    What you pull, where you pull it, and which signal you trust.

      Each pull rolls the counter down. Lever one alone: absentee 105.9, low income 62.3, stacked together 34.9 in Knox County.

### 1. Stack complementary owner signals

      Two mild signals on the same owner multiply. The combo beats both parents.

        Knox County: Absentee alone 105.9, Low Income alone 62.3, together 34.9 doors per deal.

      Combining owner situations makes the list more efficient. Stacking distress on top of the AI score does the opposite: 48.4 doors versus 30.6 for the score alone in the 922-deal backtest. The score already accounts for distress.

### 2. Move one ZIP over

      Knox runs 105 to 586 doors per deal by ZIP: a 5.6x spread inside one county.

        37914 and 37917 run at about half the county baseline. 37922 makes you work 5.6x harder for the same contract.

### 3. Trust strong signals, skip dead weight

      A bad roof is a 5.6x signal. A bad driveway is dead weight.

        Driving-for-Dollars signals, Knox + Blount metro. D4D supply is estimated and metro-level: a coverage overlay, never a one-to-one ranking.

## Where these numbers come from.

    Every figure on this page traces to one master research file. Here is its recipe.

#### The window

Six complete months, December 2025 to May 2026. Single-family, off-market, sold to an investor. Knox and Blount counties, TN.

#### The supply

Live SiftMap list counts: the exact record count each lever returns today, pulled on an account with off-market AI provisioned.

#### The deals

Sold-to-investor transactions back-tested against each list. If the property sold to an investor and sat on your list, it counts.

#### The confidence

High-confidence rows are large enough samples to act on. Thin samples sit below a SPECULATIVE divider: directional only.

    These numbers move with the window. The earlier 922-deal backtest set the curriculum band at 22.8 to 30.6 doors for AI 80 to 90; this window reads 25.3 to 32.0. The order never flips. Re-pull it quarterly.

## Six ways to misread it.

      1

#### Reading effort without payoff

10.4 doors per deal sounds elite until the median gross is negative $5k. Always read both.

      2

#### Trusting tiny samples

Downtown ZIP 37902 reads 1.7 doors per deal on a 10-record list. Six deals, no supply. Noise.

      3

#### AND-stacking on the AI score

Owner combos improve efficiency (34.9). A distress filter stacked on the score worsens it: 48.4 versus 30.6 alone.

      4

#### Ranking D4D one-to-one

D4D supply is estimated, metro-level, with no live list. It extends coverage; it does not compete on this metric.

      5

#### Confusing doors with dials

Doors are records marketed. Dials are phone attempts, a separate Day 5 KPI: about 100 correct numbers per deal.

      6

#### Ranking the funnel by one number

Notice of Default reads 13.6 but every dialer owns it. Senior reads 198.5 and keeps producing. Read saturation and cycle speed too.

## What the number tells you to do.

    The band picks the marketing mode. That is the whole point of measuring effort first.

#### Work it in precision mode

        Small list, high intent. Click-to-dial, aggressive multi-touch, every record worked by name.

        Volume · over 35

#### Work it in bulk mode

        Big list, thin signal. Power dialer, cold call first, six to eight attempts before recycling.

        Huge list, no band

#### Narrow it before anyone dials

        Cut by AI score or a second owner signal until the list is worth dialing.

#### Upgrade the touch

        Foreclosure-grade lists read sharp and dial terribly: everyone runs them. Knock the door instead.
